Hospital Patient Data Analysis System

2nd-Year B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning)

1. Project Objectives

The primary objective of this project is to architect and develop an integrated web application that enables hospital administrators to systematically store, organize, filter, and analyze clinical encounter records. By coupling a relational database (SQLite) with modern exploratory data analysis (EDA) techniques (Pandas) and interactive visualization libraries (Chart.js), the system bridges the gap between raw healthcare records and actionable operational insights.

2. Limitations of Existing Systems
Manual & Fragmented Registers

Many community clinics rely on physical ledger books or disjointed spreadsheet files with zero concurrency controls, leading to duplicate entries and human transcription errors.

Lack of Real-Time Analytics

Traditional filing systems cannot generate instant visualizations of department load, seasonal admission spikes, or patient demographics without tedious manual calculations.

Data Hygiene Vulnerabilities

Spreadsheets rarely validate data entry at runtime, allowing impossible age values, invalid calendar dates, or missing required diagnostic categories.

Privacy Exposure Risks

Unsanitized spreadsheets often mix sensitive personal identifying information (PII) with administrative records, exposing patient identities unnecessarily.

3. Proposed System & Advantages

Our proposed system implements a clean client-server architecture with strict input validation, parameterized SQL statements, automated age-group indexing, real-time Chart.js dashboards, and a dedicated Pandas data quality audit suite.

  • Automated Data Hygiene: Real-time checks for null values, duplicate entries, and out-of-bound inputs.
  • Privacy by Design: Explicit de-identification; records use randomized PID identifiers rather than patient names or phone numbers.
  • Interactive Visual EDA: Dynamic bar, line, and doughnut charts directly connected to live database state.
  • High Performance & Portability: Lightweight SQLite database with zero external server dependencies, running seamlessly on local systems.

5. AI/ML Roadmap & Future Scope
1. Length of Stay (LOS) Prediction

Train Random Forest / XGBoost regressor models on patient age, department, and diagnosis to forecast anticipated hospitalization days.

2. Readmission Risk Classifier

Deploy Logistic Regression or Support Vector Machines to classify patients at high risk of 30-day emergency readmission.

3. Bed Allocation Forecasting

Utilize ARIMA / Prophet time-series models on monthly visit trends to forecast bed and oxygen supply demands per department.
